Manual

Getting started

  • Click the power button, top-left, to start the audio engine (browsers require a click before sound can play).
  • Play notes with your mouse/touch on the keyboard, your computer keyboard, or a connected MIDI device.

Modules

  • Oscillator — choose a waveform (sine, square, sawtooth, triangle), shift octaves, dial in fine detune and glide (portamento) time.
  • Filter — a resonant low-pass filter. Cutoff sets the brightness, Resonance emphasises the cutoff frequency.
  • Envelope — classic ADSR shaping: Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release, applied to every note.
  • Spatial FX — drag the pad to move the sound source left/right and near/far in 3D space using the WebAudio PannerNode (HRTF). The knob controls elevation.
  • Master — overall output volume, plus live MIDI connection status.
  • Visualizer — a real-time oscilloscope (waveform) and spectrum analyzer, rendered on <canvas> via requestAnimationFrame, with a live FPS counter.

Controls

  • Knobs: click and drag up/down to change value. Scroll wheel over a knob also nudges it.
  • Keyboard mapping: A W S E D F T G Y H U J K = one octave, chromatic, starting at C.

Bonus features

  • Presets — save the full state of every module to your account (cloud-synced, private to you) and reload it later.
  • 3D spatial audio — the Spatial FX module positions notes in 3D space around the listener using HRTF panning.
  • MIDI keyboard support — connect a class-compliant MIDI keyboard; note on/off messages play the synth directly. If your browser or environment blocks MIDI access, the status line explains why.
